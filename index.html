How I work

The common thread is practical execution: clarify the problem, make tradeoffs visible, give teams usable requirements, and keep delivery moving through release and follow-through.

Product definition

Problem framing, user and business constraints, product briefs, requirements, user stories, acceptance criteria and prioritisation.

Delivery operations

Roadmaps, backlogs, milestones, sprint planning, RAID-style tracking, dependencies, status visibility and decision logs.

QA & release

QA/UAT, defect triage, release readiness, launch checks, live issue handling and post-release iteration.

Technical bridge

APIs, integrations, SQL/database understanding, workflow systems and implementation tradeoffs grounded in engineering experience.

Data-informed judgement

KPI interpretation, workflow friction, issue-pattern analysis, cohort and A/B-test hypothesis thinking with appropriate analytics support.

Stakeholder translation

Clear written decisions, risks, handovers and delivery updates across engineering, design, QA, product, operations and commercial stakeholders.
